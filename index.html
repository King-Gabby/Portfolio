About Me

Builder at the intersection
of AI and systems.

I'm Gabriel Ayodeji — an AI engineer and founder building production-grade intelligent systems with real-world impact. My work focuses on applied artificial intelligence and computer vision.

I've built an Automatic Number Plate Recognition (ANPR) platform, real-time hand tracking and mouse-control systems, and I'm finalising Bookiee AI — an AI-powered application built for practical deployment. I specialise in turning AI concepts into working, deployable software.

My technical stack includes Python, FastAPI, OpenCV, Streamlit, NumPy, Pandas, Matplotlib, and Angular, with hands-on experience deploying backends to cloud platforms, managing API integrations, debugging production environments, and building full-stack systems end-to-end.

Beyond engineering, I founded CelesTium, developing AI infrastructure systems beginning in emerging markets with a global outlook. I've also built ASAPTrade and created technical education programs, mentoring students in Python, AI, and foundational computing.

I don't just write code — I design architectures, validate assumptions, deploy solutions, and iterate based on real-world constraints. Currently preparing for Canada, and seeking roles in high-performance AI, backend, or computer vision teams.
